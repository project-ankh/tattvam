Questions, answered

What happens in a tarot reading?

You bring a question, cards are drawn and read in the context of your life. Expect plain, honest guidance about where you stand, what is influencing the situation and what may help. Tarot offers perspective, not fixed fate.

What is a pendulum reading?

A pendulum is a weighted crystal on a string. Its movement is read as a response to your question, which makes it well suited to quick yes-or-no answers and to checking energy.

What is an aura scan and energy check?

Your aura is the energy field traditionally understood to surround you. A scan and check looks for heaviness, blockages or imbalance before any clearing begins, so the healing is aimed at what is actually there.

What do hexes and curses mean here?

They are understood as harmful intentions or heavy energy directed at a person, place or pet. If you feel drained or weighed down for no clear reason, an energy check can show what is present. Ongoing physical or mental symptoms deserve a doctor's attention as well.

Why is a consultation needed before a spell?

Every spell is custom-crafted. Without understanding your situation, a spell would be generic, and generic spellwork helps no one.
